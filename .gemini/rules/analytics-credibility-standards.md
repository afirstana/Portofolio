# Analytics Credibility & Empirical Precision Standards

## 1. Empirical Grounding & Anti-Hyperbole Mandate
- Never fabricate statistical claims (e.g., inventing p-values or confidence intervals not present in the source analysis).
- Never frame observational correlations as causal mechanisms. Always include explicit disclaimers: "This is descriptive correlation on raw data, not a causal claim; it has not been tested experimentally."
- Avoid dramatic or hyperbolic language. Use clear, objective, professional phrasing (e.g., "Reading:", "Consistent pattern", "Plausible interpretation", "Descriptive relationship").

## 2. Structural Anomaly Transparency
- When identifying non-linear patterns or spikes (e.g., the 10x installment surge), categorize them as **hypotheses based on distribution shape**, not definitive facts, and explicitly recommend product/engineering validation.

## 3. Minimalist Presentation Architecture
- Organize case study narratives into clear, disciplined sections:
  1. Objective & Scope
  2. Data Preparation & Grain Invariants
  3. Distribution & Macro Metrics
  4. Specific Segment / Correlation Breakdown
  5. Category / Cross-Tab Comparisons
  6. Key Takeaways
  7. Business Recommendations
  8. Limitations
