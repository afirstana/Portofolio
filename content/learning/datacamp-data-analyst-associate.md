## 01. Program Objective & Context

The **DataCamp Data Analyst Associate (DAA)** certification is an industry-recognized credential designed to validate practical data analysis capabilities within a **30-day evaluation window**.

The certification assesses competency in querying relational databases under timed conditions, conducting rigorous exploratory data analysis, and presenting data-backed business recommendations to executive stakeholders.

---

## 02. Core Examination Pillars

| Assessment Pillar | Focus Dimension | Evaluated Competencies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01. Timed SQL Exam** | Relational Querying | Multi-table `JOIN`, `WINDOW` functions (`ROW_NUMBER`, `DENSE_RANK`), Common Table Expressions (`CTE`), and conditional aggregations (`CASE WHEN`). |
| **02. EDA & Statistics** | Statistical Diagnostics | Data distribution evaluation, outlier detection (`IQR`), normality diagnostics, and hypothesis testing ($t$-test / Mann-Whitney $U$). |
| **03. Practical Business Exam** | Capstone Case Delivery | End-to-end dataset cleansing, metric formulation, customer segmentation, and commercial recommendations. |
| **04. Executive Communication** | Decision Framing | Clear chart visual hierarchy, bottom-line-up-front (BLUF) executive synthesis, and reproducible notebooks. |

---

## 03. Applied Milestones & Deliverables

1. **Timed SQL Technical Assessment**: Solving relational extraction and data manipulation challenges under timed execution constraints.
2. **Exploratory Data Diagnostics**: Cleaning missing values, normalizing skewed distributions, and validating data integrity invariants.
3. **Hypothesis Formulation & Testing**: Determining statistical significance across customer segments and transaction channels.
4. **Final Business Case Submission**: Delivering a publication-grade analytical dossier complete with executive summary and reproducible code.
