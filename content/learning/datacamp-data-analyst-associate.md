## 03. Practical Exam Breakdown (FoodYum Case Study)

The practical exam required ingesting, validating, and querying raw restaurant transaction records under strict data quality rules:

1. **Task 1 (Data Validation & Cleansing)**: Checked 8 columns against categorical and numeric domain constraints, replacing missing values with category medians and standardized labels (`15/15 checks passed`).
2. **Task 2 (Comparative Analysis)**: Quantified customer dining frequency across payment channels and isolated repeat visit trajectories.
3. **Task 3 (Distribution Analysis)**: Computed percentile ranks and identified spend dispersion patterns across metropolitan vs suburban outlets.
4. **Task 4 (Business Metric Formulation)**: Formulated actionable promotional recommendations to lift off-peak weekday basket size.
